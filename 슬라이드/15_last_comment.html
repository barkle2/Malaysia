Keep Building AI In-House

Expect setbacks

Some prototypes will fail. Tools that work may see little use, and generative AI can give wrong answers.

Build on what you know

Civil servants know their work firsthand. Job stability gives them room to experiment with vibe coding.

Keep improving how we work

Even top performers may prefer familiar ways of working. Persist with changes that help the whole team work better.
