How MOEL Enabled Staff to Build AI

4. Recognize and encourage initiative

Many staff invest their own time and money in AI tools to make work easier for colleagues and improve services for the public.

Give early ideas a fair chance

Ask what problem the tool could solve and give staff time to explore the idea before judging its value.

Recognize the effort

Praise and encouragement can motivate staff to keep building. Small incentives can reinforce that recognition.
